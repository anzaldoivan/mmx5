# Prior art — leads, their status, and their credit

*psxdecomp S3 drafted this file on 2026-10-03. It was confirmed at the S4 gate on 2026-10-03. The shape is mmx6's `docs/prior-art.md` (2026-10-01).*

Every fact that this project takes from outside its own bytes is a row here, with its source, licence and status.

The status ladder is `lead` → `consistent-with-bytes` → `proven-at-gate`. `consistent-with-bytes` means checked
against our dump or an oracle. Cite the check. `proven-at-gate` means a green byte gate, or a runtime proof. Nothing
below `proven-at-gate` is banked on, recorded as fact, or used as a name. `rejected` keeps a lead that the developer
turned down, with the reason.
