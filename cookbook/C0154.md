# m2c seed preparation limits (gcc-2.95 PSX)

- Raw `m2c -t mipsel-gcc-c` output does not compile under gcc-2.95 cc1: `?` types, member access through `void*`,
  undeclared `D_` symbols. Budget a typing pass (coder repair, or `--context`) before `waves.py try` can compare bytes.
- `--context` cannot parse include/types.h directly (preprocessor directives unsupported): preprocess it first
  (`cpp -P`) or seed without context.
- Functions with jump tables fail unless the rodata table is supplied in the asm input. Such seeds are lost (W23: 2 of 12).
- Seeds are drafts only, under `.run/wave/<W>/seed/` (G12). `waves.py seed W<k>` writes them (tools/wave/README.md).
