# C0240 — s16 field tested then decremented in the arm: read it into a u16 local before the test
tags: codegen,s16,u16,lh,lhu,decrement,local,load-order,zero-test · date: 2026-10-25 · phase/task: 3.3/T29 · origin: W51 EEF0C_ovl23

symptom: an s16 field is tested for zero and decremented in the then-arm. The target loads it twice before the `beqz`, an `lh` for the test and an `lhu` for the decrement. Ours emits the `lhu` late, after the arm's other stores.

lever: read the field into a `u16` local before the test. Test the field through an s16 view, and store `n - 1` in the arm. Both loads then sit before the branch.
