# C0018 — All-asm overlays with embedded data need -march=mips4 in gas
tags: gas,mips,overlay,asm · date: 2026-10-03 · phase/task: 1.3/T3 · origin: -

All-asm overlays with embedded data can carry words gas rejects under the default march; assemble with a wider `-march` (mips4). Encodings are unchanged; the SHA check proves it.
