## Mechanism
The pass group is `cse_expr`. The expression p[i] expands to (plus (mult i 4) p), and expansion puts the MULT first. So the shifted index's register takes the sum (`addu $5,$5,$4`). Cite: `gcc/expr.c:7036 MULT` (staged tree refs/gcc-2.95.2-psx/). Map: docs/codegen-map/cse_expr.md §E4.

## Lever
