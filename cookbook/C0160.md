# Inlined helper needs the `inline` keyword: a plain `static` helper stays a call at -O2

The target may show a helper's body folded into its caller: no `jal`, and the helper's `return 0` as a `v0 = 0` block.
Then write the helper as `static inline`.
At -O2 our gcc does not inline a plain `static` function, so the call stays and every word differs.
Pair with C0155 (block-scoped temps) once the body is inlined.
Source: 3.3 T4, W26 target F3588_ovl24 (rider: dropping `inline` is load-bearing).
