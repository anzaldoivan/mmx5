# C0001 — The decomp idiom entry shape: residual, mechanism, lever, byte proof
tags: cookbook-format,idiom,residual,mechanism,lever,byte-proof · date: 2026-10-03 · phase/task: - · origin: decomp-architect

Every idiom entry in this cookbook is byte-proven on a named function and carries four parts:

1. **The residual** is what the diff looks like (the tell): the instruction, its position, the register, the count.
2. **The mechanism** is which compiler pass produces it and why, with the dump line that shows it. A verdict without a
   pass and a dump line is a hypothesis.
3. **The lever** is the C-level change that moves it, stated as a shape. A register pin is never the answer. A pin is
   a symptom. Before a lever enters the cookbook, strip it from the accepted body and recompile. About one credited
   lever in three is inert.
4. **The byte proof** is the function, the diff before and after, and the compilation that the claim survived. The
   compilation is standalone, a real translation unit or whole-binary.

Every entry has this pinned context:

- The toolchain triple is `L3-gcc2952psx-as286`. It is gcc 2.95.2-psx cc1 `-O2 -G0`, maspsx `--aspsx-version=2.86`
  and GNU as `-EL -march=r3000 -G0` (docs/ops/compiler-pin.md:9-19).
- The SDK evidence gives PsyQ 4.7. There are 11 Ps library stamps (libs 0,1,3,4,6,7,9,12,14,16,17) at
  0x80067F98-0x80091B80. The string "Library Programs (c) 1993-1997 Sony Computer Entertainment Inc." is also
  evidence. The ghidra_psx_ldr detection is pending.
- The target's calling convention and register roles are MIPS o32, as in the staged
  refs/gcc-2.95.2-psx/gcc/config/mips/mips.h. The args `$a0..$a3` are $4..$7 (:2425 GP_ARG_FIRST, :2426 GP_ARG_LAST),
  the return `$v0` is $2 (:2420 GP_RETURN) and `$sp` is $29 (:1693 STACK_POINTER_REGNUM). The callee-saved registers
  are `$s0..$s7` = $16..$23 and `$fp` = $30 (:1598 CALL_USED_REGISTERS zeros), and the rest are call-clobbered. The
  allocation order is linear (:1916 REG_ALLOC_ORDER). Density opens the callee-saved bank, not declaration order
  (docs/codegen-map/regalloc.md RC-3, T3).

Tag each entry with its symptom words, so `grep -i <symptom> cookbook/INDEX.md` finds it. The triage table entry maps a
tell to its mechanism and lever family.
