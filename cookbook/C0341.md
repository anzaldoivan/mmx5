# C0341 — 2-D strided row loop strength-reduced to an offset: give it its own loop index
tags: codegen,loop,index,strength-reduction,giv,biv,multiply,stride,2-d · date: 2026-11-14 · phase/task: 3.5/T2 · origin: W96 slus 8002C50C

symptom: a 2-D `D[i][0x9C]` row loop is strength-reduced to a running offset (`addiu +0x9C`). The target keeps the multiply form per row.

lever: give the 2-D strided loop its own index local (`j`). Do not share the u32 `i` of the other loops. A loop pair that acts as one group shares one own index (`k`). In loop.c, giv reduction depends on the biv pseudo's span.

## Instance
W96 slus func_8002C50C (c15): 18 byte-clear loops (C0216), match at try 8. Stride 80/96 rows were unaffected by the shared index.
