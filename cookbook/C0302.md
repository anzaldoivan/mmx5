# C0302 — Clamp ternary folds to MAX or loses v0: repeat the memory read in the condition and the arm
tags: codegen,ternary,clamp,max,fold,cse,v0,regalloc,slti,s8 · date: 2026-11-02 · phase/task: 3.4/T18 · origin: W83 FA490_ovl39

symptom: the target clamps a byte field with `slti` against 6, a branch, and `li v0,5` in the delay slot. The result is in `$v0`. Ours computes a MAX with `slt` against 5, or the result lands in `$a0`.

cause: fold turns `x < 6 ? 5 : x` on one local into `MAX_EXPR (x, 5)`. An if/else or a preset local keeps the branch, but local-alloc then picks another register for the result.

lever: write the ternary with the memory read in the condition and in the arm: `((s8)a[0x61] < 6) ? 5 : (s8)a[0x61]`. Fold cannot match the two reads as one operand, so it keeps the branch. CSE then merges the two loads, and the result lands in `$v0`.
