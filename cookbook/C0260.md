# C0260 — u16 field incremented then compared ==N: pre-increment inside the compare, not a temp local
tags: codegen,u16,increment,pre-increment,sh,andi,compare,delay-slot,store-order · date: 2026-10-25 · phase/task: 3.3/T37 · origin: W59 F796C_ovl09

symptom: a u16 field is incremented, stored, then compared `== N`. Ours puts the `andi 0xFFFF` before the `sh` and moves the `sh` into the `bne` delay slot. The target stores first, then masks.

lever: write the pre-increment inside the compare, `if (++*(u16*)(obj + 0x84) == 50)`. A u16 temp local (`t = field + 1` then `field = t` then `if (t == 50)`) gives the wrong order.

## Instance

Related: C0223 is the modulo case. There the increment goes on its own line, because inside the test the `sh` lands after the `multu`. Both entries agree that the u16 temp local is worse.
