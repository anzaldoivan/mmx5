# A reloc mask from our object garbles the target word at a shifted offset

When: you compare our compile with the target bytes word by word. Our object's relocation fields mask both streams
(probe.masked). Our stream is shifted against the target (length drift, reorder).

Trap: our reloc at offset X sits on a lui/addiu/jal. The target word at X may be another instruction. A LO16 mask
(0xFFFF) on an R-type target word clears rd/sa/funct and leaves `funct 0` with rs != 0: an invalid `sll` that
rabbitizer rejects. One undecodable word made plateau.py classify the whole head UNKNOWN (wall).
3.6 T3: 25 of 218 ledger heads read UNKNOWN from this alone (all target-side, all op 0 funct 0).

Move: apply a mask to the target word only when its major opcode (word >> 26) equals ours at that offset
(tools/permuter/plateau.py words()). After it: 0 UNKNOWN, classes moved to opcode-mixed, regalloc-perm, length-drift.

Check: count the undecodable words per stream by major opcode and funct. Never print bytes (G12). A target-only
cluster of one opcode at our reloc offsets is this trap.
