# C0214 — Halfword field read into a local: u16 local gives lhu, s16 gives lh, whatever the table type
tags: codegen,u16,s16,lh,lhu,local,load-sign,table · date: 2026-10-24 · phase/task: 3.3/T22 · origin: W44 slus 80052D30 8004CED0 80030D88

symptom: a halfword table or struct field is read once into a local and reused for several stores. It loads with `lh` where the target has `lhu`, or the reverse.

lever: the load sign follows the type of the local, not the type of the field. A `u16` local gives lhu. An `s16` local gives lh. Pick the local type from the target load, also when the table is declared s16.

## Instances
- W44 slus 80052D30 (c13): `extern s16 D[][4]` fields [2] and [3] read into u16 v, w. Matched on try 2.
- W44 slus 8004CED0 (c41): field 0xE in a u16 local.
- W44 slus 80030D88 (c36): field 0xA in a u16 local.
