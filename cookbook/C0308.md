# C0308 — Copy loop pointers in the wrong temps: write *dst++ in each store arm, not one dst++ after
tags: codegen,regalloc,global-alloc,post-increment,pointer,loop,if-else,cross-jump,jump2 · date: 2026-11-02 · phase/task: 3.4/T20 · origin: W85 slus 8002C088

symptom: a copy loop stores through `dst` in three if/else arms. The loop pointers and the counter are in the wrong temporary registers. The target has `dst` in `$t1`, `src` in `$t2` and `i` in `$t3`.

cause: jump2 cross-jumps the three increments back into one tail. But the extra references in the source raise the global-alloc priority of `dst`.

lever: write `*dst++ = X;` in each store arm, not `*dst = X;` with one `dst++;` after the if/else.

## Instance
W85 slus func_8002C088 (c36) matched at try 14. The `*dst++` arms took the diff from 13 words to the match. A separate `u32` loop local in place of the incremented parameter was the first lever, from 48 words to 13 (C0170 kin). Declaration order swaps and a counter for each inner loop were inert or worse. This is one datapoint.
Near: C0150 (per-site stores cross-jump into one tail), C0170.
