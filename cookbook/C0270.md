# C0270 — Byte counter decremented as addiu 0xFF: store u8 t + 0xFF, not t - 1
tags: codegen,u8,decrement,addiu,0xff,sb,spelling · date: 2026-10-31 · phase/task: 3.4/T6 · origin: W71 F52D4_ovl11 F3414_ovl03

symptom: a byte counter is decremented and stored with `sb`. The target adds `0xFF`, but ours adds `-1`. One word differs.

lever: read the byte into a `u8` local `t`. Then store `t + 0xFF`, not `t - 1`. The stored byte is the same.

## Instance

Related: C0162 (store of the constant 0xFF as -1), C0184 (a byte join variable declared s32).
