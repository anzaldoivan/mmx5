# C0268 — Pointer tested for zero twice after an init: put the alloc and init in a static inline helper
tags: codegen,inline,helper,null-test,beqz,jump-thread,spawn · date: 2026-10-31 · phase/task: 3.4/T5 · origin: W70 F9AE0_ovl04

symptom: the target tests the same pointer for zero twice, with two `beqz` on one saved register. One test comes after the allocation and one after the init stores. Two `if (p)` tests in one body lose the second `beqz`, because gcc folds or threads it away.

lever: move the allocation and the init stores into a `static inline` helper that returns the pointer. Test the pointer in the helper and again in the caller. The second `beqz` then stays.

## Instance
W70 rockx5 func_800F9AE0_ovl04 (c35), match at try 4. The helper took the diff from 80 words to 15. C0185 closed the rest.
Related: C0160 (a helper must be `static inline`), C0212 (a sibling helper keeps a constant address).
