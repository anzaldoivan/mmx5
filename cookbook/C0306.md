# C0306 — Post-call constant 1 held in a saved reg: set an s32 one = 1 after the call
tags: codegen,cse,constant,QImode,SImode,s32,local,call,callee-saved,frame · date: 2026-11-02 · phase/task: 3.4/T20 · origin: W85 EEAD8_ovl26 EED5C_ovl28

symptom: three byte stores of 1 come before a call, and one more byte store of 1 comes after it. Ours holds the 1 in `$s0` across the call and grows the frame to 0x20. The target sets `li 1` again after the call.

cause: the four constants are QImode. CSE matches the post-call 1 to the pre-call 1, so the value must live across the call.

lever: set an `s32` local just after the call, then store it. Write `func_8002C1C8(); one = 1;`, then `p[0xF4] = one;` later. CSE does not match the SImode 1 to the QImode 1.

## Instance
W85 rockx5 func_800EEAD8_ovl26 (c5) matched at try 9. The lever took the diff from 17 words to 3. W85 rockx5 func_800EED5C_ovl28 (c23) matched at try 13, with `one = 1;` set just before the store. These are two datapoints.
Do not use the `s32` local for the pre-call stores. Then the `li` moves above the `lui` chain.

A real loop between the call and the store also stops the CSE, but it leaves a loop. A `do { } while (0)` does not stop it.
In both functions, the last 3 words were a `li`/`lui` swap in `$v0` and `$v1`. A new `s32` local for 0x1F, set before the `D_800D50DE` store, fixed it. Near: C0291, C0193.

## Extension: a shared constant 2 (W86)
W86 rockx5 func_800EF74C_ovl24 (c32) is a third datapoint, with the value 2. One `s32 two = 2;` is shared by the wrap store `p[7] = two` and the compare `v == two`. The constant then stays in `$s1` (73 to 22 words). The match also needed C0196 and C0069.
