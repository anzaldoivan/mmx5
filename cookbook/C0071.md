# C0071 — Masked-signature dup group is not a byte-twin set: verify bytes in place before propagating
tags: dedup,propagate,byte-twin,masked-signature,dedup-callee · date: 2026-10-06 · phase/task: 1.8/T5 · origin: current/T5 c26

A dup group keyed by masked signature is not a byte-twin set. Its members differ in callee (jal) targets and data addresses.
Before you propagate a banked rep to its members, compare raw words in place (`propagate.twin`). Free jal targets only when the rep takes `DEDUP_CALLEE`.
Masked-only members stay ordinary asm targets (counted by `propagate.py check`), not stubs.
Origin: 1.8 T5 c26 (754 byte twins propagated and 399 masked-only returned to asm).
