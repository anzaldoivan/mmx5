## Mechanism
Pass group `cse_expr`. expand_binop (`gcc/optabs.c:802`, the commutative block) reorders the operands by RTL shape: a zero_extend/mem operand sorts ahead of a sign-extend shift, a plain SI reg sorts after it. The C operand order alone does not move it (T4.c10 tries 6-12); the operand's RTL form does.

## Lever
