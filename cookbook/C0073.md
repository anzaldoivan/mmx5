## Mechanism
The pass group is `cse_expr`. The function expand_binop (`gcc/optabs.c:802`, the commutative block) reorders the operands by RTL shape. A zero_extend/mem operand sorts ahead of a sign-extend shift, and a plain SI reg sorts after it. The C operand order alone does not move it (T4.c10 tries 6-12), but the operand's RTL form does.
