# C0118 — Auto-symbolized data near a section start is often packed byte tuples, not pointers
tags: symbols,pointers,data,false-positive,shift,scanner · date: 2026-10-12 · phase/task: 2.3/T6 · origin: generalizable

After auto-symbolizing data against a symbol feed, look at the words near a section start. A word equal to a symbol or symbol+small addend (e.g. 0x80010001) is often a packed byte tuple. Check the table neighbours and mark the table can_reference:False. A relocation-based scanner proves no missed references, never no false ones. Only a shifted build that still runs proves that.
