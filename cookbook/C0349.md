# A propagate `reloc type/addend mismatch` twin banks as a copy of its mate's C

Problem: `propagate.py sweep` refuses a masked-only twin as `reloc type/addend mismatch at word <k>` or `raw word <i> differs`.
In 3.5/T4 the 39 addend and 22 raw-word members had 3 causes:
- A switch. The C mate's jump table is a `.rodata` section reloc, and the asm names it `jtbl_<addr>`. The gate cannot rename a section.
- A data base shifted by the same delta in every symbol, or an `x+2` addend against a symbol 2 bytes later.
- A different constant or struct offset (store `+0x80` where the mate has `+0x84`, `-0x50000` against `0x50000`).

Fix: copy the mate body into the member's own C. Give it the member's symbols, constants and offsets, and read `<k>`/`<i>` first.
Run `waves.py try` and compare relocations with objdump (try masks them, C0137).
Carve it with `carve.py apply ... --body <copy>`, and carve gives the jtbl to the C file's own .rodata.
3.5/T4: 68 of 68 matched by try 1-2 at about 55-87k context per 7-13 members. The first rockx5 SHA build was green.
