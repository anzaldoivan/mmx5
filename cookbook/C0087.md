# Jump-table owner's rodata span ends at the jtbl end, not the next label
- Ending a span at the next label includes the alignment pad after a jtbl: 4 bytes too long.
- The C build emits the jtbl without the pad, so the remainder links early (first diff at the jtbl).
- End the span at label + 4*n, n = consecutive words that are addresses inside the owner; start the remainder sub there.
Origin: 1.8.2/T13.1, fixed in carve.py by T17.
