# C0242 — Two global base locals with lui/addiu pairs swapped: declare them in the target's order
tags: codegen,base-pointer,local,declaration-order,lui,addiu,global,address · date: 2026-10-25 · phase/task: 3.3/T29 · origin: W51 slus 800560D8

symptom: two global base locals get their `lui`/`addiu` pairs in the wrong order. Only the two address pairs are swapped, the rest matches.

lever: declare the base locals in the order the target materialises the addresses. The address loads follow declaration order, not the order of first use.

## Instance
W51 slus 800560D8 (c35), match at try 2. `s32* tbl = D_8007D3E8` declared before `s8* g = D_800D1C00`. Try 1 had them the other way round, 4 of 27 words off.
Related: C0185 (block-local base pointers), C0046 (declaration order breaks an s-register tie).
