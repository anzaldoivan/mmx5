# C0041 — Non-legacy INCLUDE_ASM needs macro.inc, noat/noreorder, excluded .s
tags: splat,include_asm,build · date: 2026-10-05 · phase/task: 1.6/T4 · origin: T4 gotcha

With splat `use_legacy_include_asm: False`, INCLUDE_ASM needs macro.inc plus `.set noat`/`.set noreorder` in the include. You must exclude the nonmatchings .s from the standalone asm object list (else duplicate symbols). Origin: phase-1.6 T4 (mk/slus.mk B_ASM).
