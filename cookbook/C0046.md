## Residual

## Mechanism
In pass group `regalloc`, global alloc ranks allocnos by density floor_log2(refs)*refs/live_length. On an exact tie, the tie-break is the allocno number = pseudo number = declaration order. Thus the first-declared local takes $s0. Cite: `gcc/global.c:615 v1` (staged tree refs/gcc-2.95.2-psx/). Map: docs/codegen-map/regalloc.md §A K1, §B RC-3 (README row: a temporary in a spill slot).

## Lever
Swap the two declarations and keep the statements unchanged. The lever works only on an exact density tie (unequal densities are declaration-order stable).
