# C0189 — Duplicated store in a j delay slot: write the join store once, after the if/else
tags: codegen,reorg,delay-slot,join,store,duplicate,if-else · date: 2026-10-24 · phase/task: 3.3/T17 · origin: W39 slus 800378C8

symptom: the target has the same `sb` twice, once in a then-arm's `j` delay slot and once at the join. Ours writes the store in both arms and schedules them apart.

cause: reorg fills the then-arm's `j` delay slot by copying the first insn of the join block. A store written once after the if/else shows up twice in the asm.

lever: write a store common to both arms once, after the if/else. Do not copy it into each arm.

## Instance
W39 slus 800378C8: `obj[0x84] = 0` in both inner arms gave diff 29 of 45. Written once after the inner if/else (and `obj[0x86]` after the outer join): match.
Related: C0152 (per-branch stores, the converse when the target keeps one store per arm).
