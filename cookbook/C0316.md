# C0316 — Retry loop with two exits, poll arms out of line: break on result 0, not return 0
tags: codegen,loop,break,early-return,exit,join,block-layout,retry · date: 2026-11-06 · phase/task: 3.4/T23 · origin: W88 slus 8001D90C

symptom: a retry loop calls a poll and leaves on result 0. The target joins with `beqz s0` and `move v0,s0` at one exit. Ours has an early return, and the event arms after the poll are out of line.

cause: a `return 0` in the loop makes a second exit block. A `break` keeps one exit, and the loop result goes to the shared `return result;`.

lever: in the retry loop, write `if (result == 0) { break; }`. Do not write `return 0`. Keep one `return result;` after the loop.
