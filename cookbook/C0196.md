# C0196 — Lui per arm, one sw at the join: write the join store in each arm, jump2 cross-jumps it
tags: codegen,cross-jump,jump2,join,store,duplicate,if-else,lui · date: 2026-10-24 · phase/task: 3.3/T18 · origin: W40 slus 800180DC F289C

symptom: the target has a `lui` of a global (or a `li`) in each arm and one shared `sw` at the join. Ours writes the store once after the if/else and gets one `lui` at the join.

cause: a store written at the end of each arm makes the arm tails identical. Then jump2 cross-jumps the shared `sw` into the join, and sched keeps each arm's `lui`.

lever: write the join store at the end of each arm, not once after the if/else. This is the inverse of C0189.

## Instances
W40 slus 800180DC: one `D_800E8060 = 0` after the inner if/else gave diff 21 of 43. The store in each arm matched.

W40 F289C_ovl11: one `*(s32*)(obj + 0x20) = vel` after the if/else gave diff 2 of 43. The store at the end of each outer arm matched.
Related: C0189 (the converse), C0152 (per-branch stores), C0050 (cross-jump law).
