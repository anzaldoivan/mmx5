# C0215 — Insert into the high s16 half of a reg local: Fix0418 union, write d.h.hi
tags: codegen,union,s16,store-bit-field,sll,or,sra,uninit,local,Fix0418 · date: 2026-10-24 · phase/task: 3.3/T22 · origin: W44 F1A88

symptom: an uninitialised callee-saved reg gets `andi s0,0xFFFF` in each arm. At the join it gets `sll 16` and `or`, then a `sra 16` test. This is an insert into the high s16 half of a 4-byte local in a register (store_bit_field).

lever: use a local union with an s32 member and an s16 high half. `Fix0418 d;` (include/types.h:2052) is `union { s32 w; Half0418 h; }`. Write and read only `d.h.hi`. The s32 member gives SImode, so the union lives in a reg.

Rejected:
- `Half0418` alone: 2-byte align gives BLKmode and stack sh/lh (36 of 37 words differ).
- manual `(h & 0xFFFF) | (x << 16)`: 28 of 37 words differ.
- an `s32 :16` bitfield matches, but shapes.py refuses bitfields.
- a new typedef union duplicates Fix0418.
