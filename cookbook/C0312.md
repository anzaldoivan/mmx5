# C0312 — u8 param compared with sltu, target slti: copy it to an s32 local before the compare
tags: codegen,u8,param,compare,slti,sltu,shorten_compare,local · date: 2026-11-02 · phase/task: 3.4/T22 · origin: W87 FAD04_ovl27

symptom: a `u8` parameter is compared with a small constant. The target has `slti`, but ours has `sltu`. The `andi` of the parameter also moves before the `sll` of the next parameter in the prologue.

cause: gcc shortens the compare of a `u8` value to an unsigned compare. A cast to `(s32)` in the compare does not stop it.

lever: copy the parameter to its own `s32` local first: `s32 kind; kind = arg1;`. Use the local in each compare and store.

## Instance

Near: C0173 (the reverse: cast to `u32` for `sltiu`).
