# C0314 — Call result times 4 kept in s0 with move in the delay slot: write f() * 2, then r * 2
tags: codegen,combine,scale,sll,call,s0,delay-slot,FAKE · date: 2026-11-06 · phase/task: 3.4/T23 · origin: W88 F7DE0_ovl00

symptom: a call result is scaled by 4 and kept in `$s0`. The target has `move s0,v0` in the delay slot of the call and `sll s0,s0,2` after it. An inline `r * 4` puts the product in `$v1`. A `r *= 4` statement moves the `move` before the `li`.

cause: combine merges two scales by 2 into one `sll` by 2 after the call. The pseudo then stays the call result in `$s0`, and the shift happens in place.

lever: scale the call result by 2 at the call (`r = f() * 2;`). Scale it by 2 again at the use (`r * 2 + 0x18`). Mark it `// !FAKE:`.
