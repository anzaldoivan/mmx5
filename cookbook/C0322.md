# C0322 — Abs of an s16 field coalesces x with the temp: read it into an s32 temp first
tags: codegen,abs,s16,s32,temp,coalesce,regalloc,bgez,delay-slot · date: 2026-11-07 · phase/task: 3.4/T25 · origin: W90 slus 80032A0C

symptom: an abs of an `s16` field gives the wrong registers. The target has `lh v0`, then `addu a1,v0` in the `bgez` delay slot, and the result `x` in `$a1`. Ours puts `x` in `$v1`, or gives a `bltz` and `j` layout.

cause: an `s16 t` and the `s16 x` coalesce into one pseudo. An `s32` temp has a different mode, so gcc keeps `t` and `x` apart.

lever: if an abs of an `s16` field coalesces, read it into an `s32` temp. Write `t = field; x = t; if (t < 0) x = -(u16 field);` with `s32 t` and `s16 x`.

## Instance
W90 slus func_80032A0C (c26) matched at try 14. The `s32` temp took the diff from 40 words to 1. A ternary and an if/else gave the `bltz` and `j` layout. This is one datapoint.

The last word was the operand order of an `addu`. An `s32 uy` local for the raw `u16` field fixed it.
