# C0069 — Post-call temp in $v1 not $v0: declare the ignored callee returning s32
tags: idiom,regalloc,call-value,v0-v1,return-type,reg-unused,callee-decl · date: 2026-10-05 · phase/task: current/T6 · origin: docs/codegen-map/levers.yaml regalloc_call_value_ret

symptom: a post-call temp in `$v1` where ours uses `$v0`
lever: regalloc_call_value_ret

## Residual
The C ignores the result of a `jal`. After that `jal`, the next short-lived temp (a constant for a store) sits in `$3` in the target and `$2` in ours. The tell regex (levers.yaml `regalloc_call_value_ret`) is `^\s*li\s+\$2,5\b`. It matches ours (before) and is absent in the after. The diffs are small: 2 of 31 words (rockx5 func_800F2688_ovl02), 22 of 50 (func_800F5AB4_ovl29), 5 of 71 (slus func_80053A4C).

## Mechanism
The pass group is `regalloc`. A callee declared returning `s32` expands to `call_value_internal1`, but a `void` callee is a plain `call_internal1`. When the caller drops the result, `call_value_internal1` sets `$v0` with a `REG_UNUSED` note. In local-alloc, block_alloc marks a REG_UNUSED output as dying *after* the insn (`gcc/local-alloc.c:1291` wipe_dead_reg, staged tree refs/gcc-2.95.2-psx/). So the output conflicts with the next insn's births, and the post-call pseudo cannot take `$2` and gets `$3`. Dump: `.lreg` of the after side shows `(set (reg:SI 2 v0) (call ...))` with `REG_UNUSED (reg:SI 2 v0)`, but the before side has no `$2` set.

## Lever
Declare the callee returning `s32` (or `int`) instead of `void`. The call statement stays unchanged. When ours has `$3` and the target `$2`, do the reverse. The callee's real return type is a fact about the callee. Prefer the consensus declaration from src/ (waves.py card). When the target shows the `$v1` temp, use this lever.

## Extension: s32 callee stops an arm store merge (W40)
Two arms store the same field, and one arm stores after a call. With a void callee, gcc cross-jumps the two stores into one `sb`. An s32 callee puts the post-call temp in `$v1`, so the arms differ and stay apart.

W40 rockx5 F8500_ovl29 (`obj[4] -= 2` vs `obj[4]--`) and FBC4C_ovl56 (`sh 6`) matched this way. Riders (T18.c50, s32 -> void): load-bearing on both. Also load-bearing on EF6E0_ovl01 and EFA2C_ovl01 (s32 -> void) and F564C_ovl28 (void -> s32, the reverse). Related: C0092.
