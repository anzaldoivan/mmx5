# C0069 — Post-call temp in $v1 not $v0: declare the ignored callee returning s32
tags: idiom,regalloc,call-value,v0-v1,return-type,reg-unused,callee-decl · date: 2026-10-05 · phase/task: current/T6 · origin: docs/codegen-map/levers.yaml regalloc_call_value_ret

symptom: a post-call temp in `$v1` where ours uses `$v0`
lever: regalloc_call_value_ret

## Residual
After a `jal` whose result the C ignores, the next short-lived temp (a constant for a store) sits in `$3` in the target and `$2` in ours. tell regex (levers.yaml `regalloc_call_value_ret`): `^\s*li\s+\$2,5\b` -- matches ours (before), absent in the after. Small diffs: 2 of 31 words (rockx5 func_800F2688_ovl02), 22 of 50 (func_800F5AB4_ovl29), 5 of 71 (slus func_80053A4C).

## Mechanism
Pass group `regalloc`. A callee declared returning `s32` expands to `call_value_internal1`, which sets `$v0` with a `REG_UNUSED` note when the result is dropped; a `void` callee is a plain `call_internal1`. local-alloc's block_alloc marks a REG_UNUSED output as dying *after* the insn so it conflicts with the next insn's births (`gcc/local-alloc.c:1291` wipe_dead_reg, staged tree refs/gcc-2.95.2-psx/): the post-call pseudo cannot take `$2` and gets `$3`. Dump: `.lreg` of the after side shows `(set (reg:SI 2 v0) (call ...))` with `REG_UNUSED (reg:SI 2 v0)`; the before side has no `$2` set.

## Lever
declare the callee returning `s32` (or `int`) instead of `void`, call statement unchanged. Reverse when ours has `$3` and the target `$2`. The callee's real return type is a fact about the callee: prefer the consensus declaration from src/ (waves.py card), and use this lever when the target shows the `$v1` temp.
