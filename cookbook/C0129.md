# Zero-store spacer: steer the prologue hi() register past local-alloc's fake lifetime

Symptom: a prologue runs byte stores (sb 0x5C, 0x60, 0x61). The target holds hi(D_8009A0B4) in v0. Ours holds it in v1 or an a-reg. An lbu or constant store sits next to the next `lui hi(sym)` of a tied hi/lo pair.

Cause: gcc 2.95 local-alloc gives each value a fake lifetime under INSN_SCHEDULING (refs/gcc-2.95.2-psx/gcc/local-alloc.c:1433). A value then conflicts with values born in the next insn. The tied hi/lo pair has the higher QTY_CMP_PRI and takes $2 first. The adjacent value is pushed to $3. sched1 keeps equal-priority stores in source order.

Lever: a register-free zero store of an unrelated field, such as `e->unk67 = 0;`. Put it between the two statements in the source. sched2 moves the store back to its target slot.

Byte proof: rockx5 func_800F5308_ovl27, W21 seed `diff 7 of 71`. Moving `e->unk67 = 0;` to just after `e->unk14 = D_8009A0B4;` gives `match`. The harvest rider is load-bearing (2.8 T10).

Same family, banked: func_800EF094_ovl03, func_800F6864_ovl26, func_800EFDDC_ovl09. A single-statement move sweep finds the order in under a minute per target. Refines C0051, C0052, C0053 and C0076.
