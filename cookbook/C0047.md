## Residual
The residual is `$s0`/`$s1` swapped, unequal use counts. The tell regex (levers.yaml `regalloc_density_order`) is `^\s*addu\s+\$16,\$4,1$`. It matches ours (before) and is absent in the after.

## Mechanism
In pass group `regalloc`, allocno_compare orders allocnos by density. The pass allocates the denser pseudo first, and that pseudo opens $s0 ($16). The next one opens $s1. Cite: `gcc/global.c:593 allocno_compare` (staged tree refs/gcc-2.95.2-psx/). Map: docs/codegen-map/regalloc.md §A K2, §B RC-3 (README rows: phantom callee-saved and `$s0`/`$s1` swapped).

## Lever
Move the use count, not the declaration. Here `a*a*a + b` vs `a + b*b*b` flips which local is densest. Reshape refs or live ranges until the target's local outranks.
