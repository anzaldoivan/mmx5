# C0223 — u16 field incremented and tested modulo: increment on its own line, then test the field
tags: codegen,u16,increment,sh,andi,multu,modulo,store-order · date: 2026-10-24 · phase/task: 3.3/T23 · origin: W45 slus 80057BD4

symptom: a u16 field incremented and tested `% 5 == 0`. The target stores the unmasked sum (`sh`) first, then the `andi` feeds the `multu`. Written inside the test, `if ((u16)++*q % 5 == 0)`, gcc puts the `sh` after the `multu`.

lever: increment on its own line, then test the field: `(*q)++; if (*q % 5 == 0)`. A u16 local copy (`c = *q + 1; *q = c;`) is worse: the `andi` comes before the `sh`.
