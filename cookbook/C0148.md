# Batched carve bisect: slus before rockx5 (rockx5 links slus)

`mk/rockx5.mk:41` links the slus build into rockx5. A slus carve that breaks the slus SHA also reds the rockx5 build.
So a batched sweep builds and bisects slus first and freezes its green set. Only then does it bisect rockx5.
Otherwise a slus loser shows up as a rockx5 SHA failure. `propagate.py sweep` does this (3.1/T2).
