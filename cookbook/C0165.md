# C0165 — Two-constant field select: if/else with a store per arm, not a ternary
tags: codegen,ternary,if-else,lui,delay-slot,select,store · date: 2026-10-20 · phase/task: 3.3/T6 · origin: W28 F03EC

symptom: a store of one of two constants (`x ? K : -K`) puts its two `lui` in the wrong slots. Ours fills a delay slot differently.
lever: write the select as if/else with one store per arm, not a ternary

## Residual
`obj->x20 = obj->x15 ? 0x40000 : -0x40000;` gcc 2.95 lowers the ternary into one join store and schedules the two `lui` differently. W28 F03EC_ovl01 diff 5 of 44 at +0x88..+0x98.

## Lever
`if (obj->x15 == 0) obj->x20 = -0x40000; else obj->x20 = 0x40000;`. Each arm keeps its own `lui` + `sw`, and the target's lui-in-delay-slot placement falls out.
Converse of C0080 (there the condition ternary is the matching form). Try both forms when a two-constant select diffs.

## Byte proof
Rider (T6.c50, `harvest.py rider`, if/else turned back into the ternary): load-bearing on rockx5 func_800F03EC_ovl01 (banked W28).

## Extension: a select of 0x8000 (W86)
W86 slus func_800373D0 (c28) is a second datapoint. A value ternary `v = c ? -0x8000 : 0x8000` hoisted `li 0x8000` into the prior delay slot (50 words). A ternary store gave the same code.
An if/else with its own `*(s32*)(obj + 0x20) =` store in each arm gave the match at try 3.
Rider (T21.c50, `harvest.py rider`, strip = the ternary store): load-bearing on slus func_800373D0 (banked W86).
