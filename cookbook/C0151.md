symptom: target has `slti; bnez; slti; beqz` (two compares), ours has `addiu -lo; sltu (hi-lo)` (one folded range check)

## Mechanism
gcc folds a two-sided range test on one value inside one condition (`&&` or `||`) into `(unsigned)(d - lo) < (hi - lo)`. It emits that as `addu`/`sltu`.
Two separate `if` statements are two conditions. fold never sees them together.

## Lever
Write each bound as its own early return: `if (d < 0x20) return; if (d >= 0x121) return;`. Do the reverse (one `||` or `&&`) when the target shows the `addu/sltu` form.
