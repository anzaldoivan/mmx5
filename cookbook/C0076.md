# C0076 — Constant stores alternate v0/v1: interleave a zero store to keep v0
tags: idiom,regalloc,v0-v1,constant-store,li-sb,local-alloc,store-order · date: 2026-10-06 · phase/task: 1.8.2/T5 · origin: docs/codegen-map/levers.yaml regalloc_const_store_interleave

symptom: constant `li;sb` pairs alternating `$v0`/`$v1` (or a third reg) where the target reuses `$v0`
lever: regalloc_const_store_interleave

## Residual
The residual is a run of constant byte stores (object init prologue). In ours, consecutive constant temps take different hard regs, but the target reuses `$2`. The tell regex (levers.yaml `regalloc_const_store_interleave`) is `^\s*li\s+\$5,1\b`. It matches the before and is absent in the after. The diff of rockx5 func_800F6E78_ovl04 with two constant stores made adjacent is 9 of 335 words.

## Mechanism
The pass group is `regalloc`. The INSN_SCHEDULING lifetime extension of local-alloc (`gcc/local-alloc.c:1415`) makes a qty conflict with qtys born or dying next to it. So a constant temp cannot take the hard reg freed by the immediately preceding insn, and back-to-back constant stores alternate regs. A `$0` store between them puts an insn in the gap, and the reg is reusable. Later, sched2 lifts the li->sb chains above the plain stores, so the visible order differs from the source order.

## Lever
Interleave a zero (or shared-constant) store between consecutive constant stores. Order the statements so that such a store separates every constant store from the next. The visible asm order is not the source order (sched2).
