# C0300 — Byte loads merged by CSE, target loads twice: sum them as (u16)(a + b) into an s32 local
tags: codegen,cse,u16,HImode,fold,combine,nonzero-bits,load,reload,sum · date: 2026-11-01 · phase/task: 3.4/T17 · origin: W82 slus 8002E274

symptom: the target loads the same two bytes twice, once for a sum and once for the compares. Ours loads them once, because CSE merges the loads. A mask on the sum, or a narrow local, adds an `andi` or swaps the `addu` operands.

cause: `(u16)(a + b)` makes fold shorten the add to HImode. The HImode loads do not match the SImode loads, so CSE keeps both. Then combine drops the `u16` mask. The nonzero bits of the sum fit in 16 bits.

lever: write the sum as `sum = (u16)(h1[2] + h2[2]);` into an `s32` local. Keep the other uses as plain reads (`w1 = h1[2];`).
