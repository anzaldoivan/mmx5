# C0194 — Byte reload cse removes: one marked volatile access, not the empty asm (gate refuses it)
tags: codegen,cse,reload,volatile,alias,FAKE,gate,lbu · date: 2026-10-24 · phase/task: 3.3/T17 · origin: W39 F4C20 F9520 T17.c49

symptom: the target reloads a byte (`lbu`/`lb`) after a store gcc proves does not alias it. Ours reuses the register from the earlier load or decrement (cse).

cause: gcc's alias analysis proves the store disjoint (different offsets of one base), so cse keeps the old value. The original was likely compiled with a barrier we cannot see.

lever: make the one reloaded access volatile, `((volatile u8*)p)[off]`, and mark the line `// !FAKE: volatile forces the reload`. Put the volatile on the access the target reloads, not on the earlier read.

## Gate
The C0058 empty asm also forces the reload. But debt kind `asm` counts `down`, and a `// !FAKE:` marked empty asm still refuses the bank commit. The marked volatile access passes. Prefer it for cse-reload cases (T17.c49 replaced F4C20's asm with it).
A non-fake lever is still open for both instances.

## Instance
W39 F4C20_ovl11: `lbu 0x83` reload after `--p[0x83] & 1` and a `sh 0xE`. W39 F9520_ovl09: byte 5 tested, then `++` after `sh 0x56` (volatile on the test read gives lbu for the test, 1 of 45).
Related: C0058 (empty asm flush), C0183, C0168.

## Byte proof
Riders (T17.c50, `harvest.py rider`, strip = plain access): load-bearing on rockx5 func_800F4C20_ovl11 and func_800F9520_ovl09 (banked W39).

## Extension: a loop-invariant load (W81)
loop.c hoists a load out of a loop when no store in the loop can change it. The target reloads it in each pass. Make the access in the loop volatile: `*(volatile u16*)&c->flags & (0x1000 << i)`, marked `// !FAKE:`. This differs from the sched1 hoist of C0294, which a volatile read does not stop.

W81 slus func_80053E3C (c7): the volatile access took the diff from 14 to 6 words. The function matched at try 19.
Rider (T16.c50, `harvest.py rider`, strip = plain `c->flags` read): load-bearing on slus func_80053E3C (banked W81).

## Extension: a u16 counter and a u8 call argument (W83)
W83 has 2 more datapoints. In rockx5 func_800EF148_ovl07 (c4), `((volatile u16*)obj)[0x9C / 2] & 7` gives the second `lhu` of the counter. In rockx5 func_800F10FC_ovl05 (c20), `((volatile u8*)obj)[2] >> 4` gives the reload for the 4th call argument.
Riders (T18.c50, `harvest.py rider`, strip = the plain access): load-bearing on both (banked W83).
