# C0182 — s16 field decrement-and-test stores late: update the field in place and test the field, no temp
tags: codegen,s16,decrement,sign-extend,sll,sra,store-order,temp,in-place · date: 2026-10-24 · phase/task: 3.3/T14 · origin: W36 F2190 F9F8C

symptom: an s16 field is decremented, stored back and tested. Ours sign-extends (sll/sra) before the `sh`, or sinks the `sh` into the branch delay slot. The target stores first, then shifts to test. An s16 temp (store the temp, test the temp) gave diff 3 in W36 F2190 and 9 words in F9F8C (try 1 each). A `u16` temp instead adds `andi 0xffff` (try 2: 14 and 3).

lever: no temp. Update the field in place and test the field: `*(s16*)(p + 0xA) -= 8; if (*(s16*)(p + 0xA) < 0xC1)` or `if (--*(s16*)(p + 0x7C) == 0)`. CSE folds the re-read, the `sh` lands before the sll/sra.

Differs from C0158 (u16 field, s32 temp + `(s16)t` test): there the temp is the lever. Here a temp is the residual.
Related: C0158, C0089/C0093 (s16 lh vs lhu).
