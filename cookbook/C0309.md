# C0309 — Bit test gives srl/andi, target andi+sltu: put the mask in its own s32 local
tags: codegen,compare,flag,andi,sltu,srl,mask,local,do_store_flag · date: 2026-11-02 · phase/task: 3.4/T21 · origin: W86 slus 80041C80

symptom: a single-bit flag test kept in a local comes out as `srl` and `andi 1`. The target has `andi 0x40` and then `sltu` against zero.

cause: tree-level `do_store_flag` turns `(x & pow2) != 0` into a shift and a mask when it sees the whole expression. If the mask is in its own local, the compare sees a plain register and emits `sltu`.

lever: put the mask in its own `s32` local, then compare that local. Write `m = flags & 0x40; go = m != 0;`.

## Instance
W86 slus func_80041C80 (c16) matched at try 4. The one-expression form gave the shift. `go = flags & 0x40; go = go != 0;` on one local gave `andi` and `sltu`, but the registers moved (9 words). A separate local `m` gave the match. This is one datapoint.
