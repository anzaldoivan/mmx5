## Residual

## Mechanism
Pass group `cse_expr`; cse reuses an identical expression within the block; an operand-less, clobber-less volatile asm is a raw ASM_OPERANDS at which cse flushes its whole table (basic `asm("")` and a "memory" clobber do not flush register classes). Cite: `gcc/cse.c:7697 flush_hash_table` (staged tree refs/gcc-2.95.2-psx/). Map: docs/codegen-map/cse_expr.md K§1-g.

## Lever
insert `__asm__ __volatile__("" : : );` between the occurrences (zero bytes) to force the recompute; prefer a re-tie (cse_call_retie) when only one value must recompute.
