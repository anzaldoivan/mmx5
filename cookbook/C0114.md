# C0114 — binutils 2.42 --just-symbols sign-extends MIPS ELF32 0x80 addresses
tags: binutils,ld,just-symbols,mips,r_mips_26 · date: 2026-10-12 · phase/task: 2.3/T4 · origin: generalizable

With binutils 2.42, `--just-symbols` on a MIPS ELF32 sign-extends 0x80xxxxxx values, and R_MIPS_26 against them fails to link. Emit the same names as linker-script assignments (`name = 0x80...;`) instead.
