# C0221 — Countdown clear loop with li and addu swapped: init counter then pointer in the for header
tags: codegen,loop,for,init,comma,sched,li,addu,byte-clear · date: 2026-10-24 · phase/task: 3.3/T23 · origin: W45 EF1E0

symptom: a byte-clear countdown loop with a pointer and a counter. The loop body matches, but `li 15` and the pointer's `addu` are scheduled in swapped order (2 words differ).

lever: init both in the for header, counter first: `for (i = 15, p = a0 + 0x35; i >= 0; i--) *p-- = 0;`. The init order sets the order of `li` and `addu`. Here `i` first puts the `li` before the global loads and the `addu` after them.
