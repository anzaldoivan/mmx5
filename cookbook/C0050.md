## Residual
The residual is a cross-jumped tail merged or not merged. The tell regex (levers.yaml `xjump_tail_merge`) is `^\s*sw\s+\$2,4\(\$5\)\n(?:\s*\.set.*\n)*\s*j\s+\$31`. It matches ours (before) and is absent in the after.

## Mechanism
In pass group `cse_expr`, jump2 turns the then-arm's jump-to-epilogue into a RETURN. RETURN-to-RETURN cross-jumping needs a 2-insn match. Thus a 1-insn shared tail stays duplicated in both arms with two returns. Cite: `gcc/jump.c:2020 find_cross_jump` (staged tree refs/gcc-2.95.2-psx/). Map: docs/codegen-map/cse_expr.md §E3.

## Lever
Write the shared tail once after the join (one copy, then-arm jumps over the else arm). When the target keeps two copies, duplicate the shared tail in each arm instead. Shape the shared block's insn count.
