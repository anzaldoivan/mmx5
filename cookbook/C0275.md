# C0275 — Shared far tail with a magic divide: goto after the duplicated divide store stops sched1 hoist
tags: codegen,goto,FAKE,magic,divide,sched,cross-jump,jump2,regalloc,block-layout · date: 2026-10-31 · phase/task: 3.4/T7 · origin: W72 EEFE0_ovl12

symptom: two arms share a far tail that starts with a magic-divide store and call setup. Ours schedules the call-argument moves and constants above `d << 16`, so `d` takes `t0`. The target has `d` in `a0`.

lever: duplicate the magic-divide store in both arms. Put the arm-1 `goto` after its store, and put the label after the same store in arm 2. Mark the goto `!FAKE`.

## Mechanism
The block boundary after the `sw 0x20` keeps sched1 from hoisting the call setup above the divide. After reload, jump2 cross-jumps the two identical divide tails into one.
