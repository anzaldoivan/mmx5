# C0323 — Lui/addiu or a counter init in the wrong slot: move the assignment next to the slot it fills
tags: codegen,reorg,delay-slot,statement-order,base-pointer,call,early-return,lui,addiu · date: 2026-11-07 · phase/task: 3.4/T26 · origin: W91 80022D98, F6A7C_ovl06

symptom: a `lui`/`addiu` pair or a counter init sits in the prologue or before a branch. The target puts it in a call delay slot or a `beqz` delay slot.

cause: reorg fills a delay slot with an earlier independent insn. The source position of an assignment sets which slots its insns can reach. An assignment at the declaration is hoisted into the prologue.

lever: if the insn is in the wrong slot, move its assignment in source. Put it just before the call or branch whose slot it fills.

## Instances
W91 slus func_80022D98 (c30) matched at try 3. With `p = D_8009A0A0` at the declaration, the `lui`/`addiu` went to the prologue (17 words). After the first call, the `lui` stood alone (55 words). After the second call, the `lui` filled the slot of call 2 and the `addiu` filled the slot of call 3.

W91 rockx5 func_800F6A7C_ovl06 (c40) matched at try 9. `i = 0;` after the `y < 0x170` early return fills the `beqz` delay slot. Before the test, or in a comma form, the `i` and `x` registers swapped (8 words).
Near: C0295 (base assignment in a loop body), C0049 (eager fill of a branch slot).

## Extension: a counter init per arm (W92)
W92 slus func_8001AE84 (c40) is a datapoint. If `i = 0` is before the test, gcc puts the `addu` before the `lbu` and a shared `lui` fills the `beqz` slot. Write `i = 0` in each arm, with the then-arm init before its loop and the fall-through init after the early return.
