# Signed zero test of a u8 byte: read it through (s8*) to keep lb

`if ((s8)p[3] != 0)` on a `u8*` compiles to `lbu`: the cast is folded, since the sign does not matter to a zero test. When the target has `lb`, read the byte through a signed pointer: `if (((s8*)p)[3] != 0)`.
