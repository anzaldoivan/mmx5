# C0290 — Bitfield insert constants out of order: plain-C insert with a redundant second mask
tags: codegen,bitfield,mask,insert,addPrim,constant,hoist,shapes,FAKE · date: 2026-11-01 · phase/task: 3.4/T14 · origin: W79 slus main

symptom: a 24-bit field is inserted into a word, as in the libgpu `addPrim` macro. The hoisted constants come in the wrong order (`0xFF000000` before `0xFFFFFF`), and the loads do not follow the target order. A `u32 addr:24` bitfield struct matches, but `shapes.py` refuses bitfields.

cause: a bitfield store masks the value again inside the insert. The plain-C form `(*q & 0xFF000000) | t` has one mask fewer. Then gcc builds a different RTL shape and orders the constants differently.

lever: write the insert in plain C with the second mask, also when the value is already masked. Mark the second mask `// !FAKE:`.
`t = src & 0xFFFFFF; *q = (*q & 0xFF000000) | (t & 0xFFFFFF);`

## Instance
W79 slus main (c43). The bitfield struct matched at try 10. The plain-C form with the second mask matched at try 16 and passes `shapes.py`.
It may also help other drafts that `shapes.py` refuses for a bitfield type.
Near: C0139 (a bitfield as an explicit mask with member stores).

## Byte proof
Rider (T14.c50, `harvest.py rider`, strip = `| t;` without the second mask): load-bearing on slus main (banked W79).
