# C0030 — psyq _OBJ_<off> symbols are labels unless preceded by a terminator or padding
tags: psyq,symbols,splat,boundaries · date: 2026-10-05 · phase/task: 1.5/T2.1 · origin: -

psyq signature symbols at object offsets (`<OBJ>_OBJ_<off>`) mark in-function labels as well as function starts. Seeding them all as `type:func` invents functions (slus: 354). Rule: demote to `type:label` any such symbol not preceded by a terminator (`jr ra`+delay, tail `j`) or padding. Origin: phase 1.5 T2/T2.1, tools/build/seed_symbols.py.
