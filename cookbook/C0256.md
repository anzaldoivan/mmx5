# C0256 — m2c seed passes a1 that only feeds a store: not an argument, drop it from the callee decl
tags: m2c,seed,callee-decl,prototype,argument,a1,delay-slot,regalloc · date: 2026-10-25 · phase/task: 3.3/T34 · origin: W56 slus 8001E810 F85AC_ovl29

symptom: the m2c seed passes a value in a1 to a callee. In the target, a1 holds only a store temp or a constant for a store in the jal delay slot.

lever: drop that argument. Declare the callee with only the params the target sets for the call, such as `extern void func_8001A028(s32)`. a1 is just the next free register.

## Instances
W56 slus func_8001E810 (c27), match at try 5. The seed guessed `func_8001A028(1, 0x80105000)`, and the constant is only the `D_800D1F38` store.
W56 rockx5 func_800F85AC_ovl29 (c45), match at try 1. The seed passed the `src + 0xC` copy temp in a1, and the callee takes obj only.

Related: C0199 (a0 passed through to a table call), C0201 (callee params s32), C0154 (seed limits).

## Byte proof
Riders (T34.c50, `harvest.py rider`, strip = the seed a1 argument kept): load-bearing on slus func_8001E810 and rockx5 func_800F85AC_ovl29 (banked W56).
