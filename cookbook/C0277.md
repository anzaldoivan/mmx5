# C0277 — Word RMW pair loads in the wrong order: move the byte store below the pair
tags: codegen,sched,store-order,rmw,sb,v0-v1,load-order,statement-order · date: 2026-10-31 · phase/task: 3.4/T7 · origin: W72 F5FA0_ovl29

symptom: two word read-modify-writes (`*= 2` on fields 0x20 and 0x24) load in the wrong order, or with `v0` and `v1` swapped. A byte store sits just before the pair. Neither source order of the pair nor a local split fixes it.

lever: move the byte store below the pair. Write `0x20 *= 2; 0x24 *= 2;`, then `obj[0x16] = 0;`.

## Instance
W72 rockx5 func_800F5FA0_ovl29 (c44) matched at try 10. With the `sb` before the pair, tries 4-9 kept the wrong load order or registers. The move took the diff from 4 to 0 words.
