# C0224 — Global halfwords at fixed offsets with hi anchored at D+off: declare s16 D[] and index it
tags: codegen,extern,s16,array,index,hi,lui,global,offset-cast · date: 2026-10-24 · phase/task: 3.3/T23 · origin: W45 EFAC8

symptom: s16 halfwords of a global read and written at fixed offsets. With `u8 D[]` and `*(s16*)(D + 0xA)` casts, the `%hi` anchors at D+10 and the registers and prologue order are off (19 of 34 words differ).

lever: declare the global `extern s16 D[];` and index it: `D[5]`, `(u16)D[0x18]`, `D[0xE]`. The `%hi` stays on the plain symbol, and the other global's load comes first.

## Instance
W45 rockx5 EFAC8_ovl04 (c44), matched on try 5. Other declarations of D_8009A1F8 are `u8[]` or `D_8009A1F8_t`, so this one is local to the draft.
Related: C0187, C0190.
