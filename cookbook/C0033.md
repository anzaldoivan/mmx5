# C0033 — Mask relocated fields by instruction field for duplicate detection
tags: census,duplication,mips,relocation · date: 2026-10-05 · phase/task: 1.5/T3 · origin: -

For duplicate detection with no relocations on the target side, mask relocated fields by instruction field. The fields are j/jal target26, lui imm16, and imm16 of addiu/ori/load/store whose rs was lui-set earlier in the function or is $gp. Spot-check dup groups against the disassembler's %hi/%lo/label operands. Origin: phase 1.5 T3, tools/census/census.py.
