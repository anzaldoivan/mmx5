# C0188 — Gate debt floor: unmarked goto refuses the bank commit, so mark it !FAKE in src and draft
tags: workflow,gate,debt-floor,goto,FAKE,bank,commit · date: 2026-10-24 · phase/task: 3.3/T16 · origin: T16.c49 W38 gate

workflow: the gate's debt floor counts unmarked `goto` lines. A banked draft can keep a `goto` for the matching branch layout. Then the gate raises `goto-unmarked` and refuses the bank commit.
fix: mark each goto line `// !FAKE: goto kept for the matching branch layout`, in src and in the draft. Then re-try the drafts (match) and re-commit.
pre-gate: grep the wave's drafts for `goto` before the gate and mark them. A coder note "uses goto" is not the full list.

## Instance
T16.c49 (W38 rockx5 gate): goto-unmarked went from 21 to 26 and refused the commit. The gotos were in F1358_ovl07 (2), F9B74_ovl28 (2) and F26BC_ovl29 (1). The brief had predicted only two of the three drafts.
The lines were marked and re-tried (match), then committed in b534d6b1.
