# C0112 — spimdisasm keeps jump-table words raw when targets are typed label symbols
tags: spimdisasm,jtbl,jump-table,symbols · date: 2026-10-12 · phase/task: 2.3/T2 · origin: generalizable

When jump-table targets are declared as `type:label` symbols, spimdisasm emits the jtbl words as raw numbers. Leave targets unnamed so it emits `.L` jlabels and relocations.
