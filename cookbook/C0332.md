# C0332 — Nested loop step and index in the wrong saved regs: step in the for-increment, index at the body end
tags: codegen,loop,for,increment,step,index,regalloc,saved-reg,statement-order · date: 2026-11-07 · phase/task: 3.4/T28 · origin: W93 slus 80028C10

symptom: two nested loops step a coordinate by a constant and count with a separate index. The target holds the stepped coordinate and the next index in saved registers in another order. It also puts the `addiu` before the `lhu` of the bound.

cause: the order of the statements sets the order of the pseudos. The order of the pseudos then sets the saved registers and the schedule.

lever: if the step and the index are in the wrong registers, put the step in the for-increment. Put the index increment at the end of the body: `for (i = 0; i < n; x += 0x10) { f(x); i++; }`.
