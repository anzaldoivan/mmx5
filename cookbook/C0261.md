# C0261 — Hand-asm signs in the target ($sp write, lone break, old-pointer loop compare): ledger at once, no C form
tags: codegen,hand-asm,include_asm,ledger,sp,break,loop,m2c · date: 2026-10-25 · phase/task: 3.3/T38 · origin: W60 slus 80013348 8001336C 80013438 8001344C

symptom: a draft stays far from the target after one or two tries. The target asm shows a sign that gcc never emits from C.

lever: if the target shows one of these signs, ledger it at once as INCLUDE_ASM. Do not spend more tries on a C form.
- The body writes `$sp`, for example a stack switch to the scratchpad (`$sp = 0x1F800400`) and its restore half.
- The body is a lone `break` with no divide around it, then `jr ra`. This is not the aspsx divide trap of C0023.
- A loop compares the old pointer with the end and increments it in the delay slot. gcc 2.95.2 always copies the pointer first with `move`.

A failed m2c seed alone is not a sign. In W60, slus func_80013388 failed in m2c, but a plain `do`/`while` word copy matched at try 1.

## Instance
W60 slus func_80013348 and func_8001336C (c16, c28) are the stack switch pair. W60 slus func_8001344C (c42) is the lone `break` stub.
W60 slus func_80013438 (c36) is the zero loop. Its neighbours in the same unit have the same hand-written style. All four failed at the W60 gate with a `body:` size verdict.
