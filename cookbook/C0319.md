# C0319 — Two sums compared in a loop, registers coloured wrong: accumulate each sum in place first
tags: codegen,regalloc,sum,accumulate,compare,local,loop,colouring · date: 2026-11-06 · phase/task: 3.4/T24 · origin: W89 F2F28_ovl06

symptom: a loop compares two summed distances. The target has the right instructions, but the loop registers are coloured differently.

cause: each `+=` reuses the pseudo of its left operand. A compare of two fresh sums makes two new pseudos. The new pseudos change the register tie-breaks for the whole loop.

lever: if a compare uses two sums, accumulate each sum in place first. Write `dx += dy; tx += ty; if (tx > dx)`, not `if (tx + ty > dx + dy)`.

## Instance
W89 rockx5 func_800F2F28_ovl06 (c14) matched at try 13. Tries 1-12 differed only in register colouring, at 29-38 words. The accumulate in place and the `tx > dx` compare closed the last 22 words. This is one datapoint.

The declaration order of the locals is not proven. Two other orders give the same bytes.
