# C0167 — shapes.py refuses a struct pointing to its own tag: type the field void*
tags: harness,shapes,struct,self-reference,void-pointer,draft · date: 2026-10-20 · phase/task: 3.3/T6 · origin: W28 F0D34 F13BC

symptom: `shapes.py check` refuses a draft struct with `unknown type struct <Tag>` although the draft compiles and matches
lever: type the self-pointer field `void*`, as include/types.h does (`void *unk7C; /* the parent */`). Give the locals the struct type.

## Gotcha
shapes.py does not resolve a struct field that points to its own tag (`struct Obj* unk50`), in the inline-tag form and in the forward-typedef form.
`void*` has the same codegen, and typed locals (`Ent* q = e->parent;`) need no cast.
Seen in W28 F0D34_ovl00 (c39) and F13BC_ovl12 (c40). Both matched with `void*`.
