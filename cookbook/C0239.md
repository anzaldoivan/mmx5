# C0239 — Callee result modulo a constant gives multu/srl: declare the callee returning u32
tags: codegen,unsigned,u32,modulo,multu,callee-decl,return-type · date: 2026-10-25 · phase/task: 3.3/T28 · origin: W50 F4818_ovl06

symptom: a callee's result taken modulo a constant gives `multu` and `srl` in the target. With the s32 callee, ours gives the signed magic sequence with its sign fixup.

lever: declare the callee as returning `u32` and keep its result in a `u32` local. The `%` is then unsigned, as the target's `multu` shows.
