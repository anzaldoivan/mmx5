# C0181 — Scratchpad pointer added into an integer sum: cast the addr.h view to s32, cast the total once
tags: codegen,addu,operand-order,scratchpad,addr-h,pointer-arith,cast · date: 2026-10-24 · phase/task: 3.3/T13 · origin: W35 F9A88

symptom: an indexed address sums a byte*2, a half and the scratchpad pointer. Ours reorders the addu chain (W35 F9A88 try 2: diff 8 of 19). The target adds in source order (lbu*2, lhu, lw).
lever: keep the sum integer-typed. Cast the addr.h view to s32 inside the sum, and cast the total to the pointer once: `((u8 *)(D_800D1DBC * 2 + D_80091D58 + (s32)D_1F800004))[0x15] = v;`

With the scratchpad term as a pointer operand (`... + D_1F800004`), gcc builds pointer arithmetic and the addu operands come in another order. The raw `*(s32*)0x1F800004` form matches but shapes refuses raw address casts (C0099), so use the addr.h name with the `(s32)` cast.
Related: C0099/C0100 (addr.h views), C0073/C0059 (addu operand order).
