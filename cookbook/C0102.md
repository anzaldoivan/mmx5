# C0102 — PS1 disc rebuild: dumpsxiso -s + mkpsxiso 2.30 round trip is byte-exact
tags: disc,mkpsxiso,repack · date: 2026-10-11 · phase/task: 2.1/T2 · origin: -

For a PS1 disc rebuild, try `dumpsxiso -s` (layout XML) + `mkpsxiso` 2.30 unmodified first, sources pointed at the extraction.
For MMX5 the round trip is byte-exact on the whole track: EDC/ECC, PVD, dates, XA/mixed files, the trailing `<dummy>` zero sectors. No post-pass needed.
Source: phase 2.1 T2 (tools/repack/disc.py, docs/formats.md `## Image rebuild (inverse)`).
