# C0311 — Divide by 0x1F00 gets the wrong magic: write (u32)(t*k) / 31 >> 8 with k an s32 local
tags: codegen,divide,magic,unsigned,srl,combine,mult,constant,local · date: 2026-11-02 · phase/task: 3.4/T22 · origin: W87 F2D44_ovl20

symptom: an unsigned divide by 0x1F00 comes out with the wrong magic number or a pre-shift. The target has the magic `0x08421085`, then `subu`, `srl 1`, `addu` and one `srl 12`. A literal multiplier also turns the `mult` into shifts and adds.

cause: gcc picks the magic for the whole divisor. A divide by 31 followed by `>> 8` gives the divide-by-31 magic, and combine merges the two shifts into `srl 12`. A literal multiplier is a constant at expand time, so gcc synthesizes it. An `s32` local is a pseudo at expand time, so the `mult` stays.

lever: write the divide as `(u32)(t * k) / 31 >> 8`, not `/ 0x1F00`. Put the multiplier in an `s32` local: `k = 0xA00;`.
