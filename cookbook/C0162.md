# C0162 — Byte store of 0xFF: write (s8*) = -1 to get addiu -1, not 0xff
tags: codegen,s8,store,constant,sb,0xff · date: 2026-10-20 · phase/task: 3.3/T5 · origin: W27 80036E60

symptom: ours `addiu $v0,$zero,0xff` (or `li 0xff`) before an `sb`, target `addiu $v0,$zero,-1`
lever: store through `(s8*)` with `-1`

## Residual
A byte store of all-ones written as `u8 = 0xFF` loads the constant 0xFF. The target loads -1. One word differs. W27 slus func_80036E60 (+0x40).

## Lever
Spell the store `*(s8*)(obj + off) = -1` (or give the field type `s8`). The stored byte is the same. Only the constant's spelling changes.
Load-side analogue: C0157 (`(s8*)` read keeps `lb`).

## Byte proof
Rider (T5.c50, `harvest.py rider`, `*(s8*) = -1` -> `obj[0x67] = 0xFF`): load-bearing on slus func_80036E60 (banked W27).

## Extension: other negative constants (W87)
The same lever works for other negative byte constants. In slus func_80052E44 (c35), `((s8*)o)[0x60] = -8` gave `addiu -8`. In rockx5 func_800F35C8_ovl20 (c39), `*(s8*)arg0 = -0x6D` gave `addiu -0x6D`. Draft rule 10 says the same.
Riders (T22.c50, `harvest.py rider`, strip = the unsigned store, 248 and 0x93): load-bearing on both (banked W87).
