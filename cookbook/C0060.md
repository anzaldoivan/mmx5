## Residual
The residual is a load stuck below a store while other loads float. The tell regex (levers.yaml `alias_struct_grant`) is `^\s*sw\s+\$5,4\(\$4\)\n(?:.*\n)*?\s*lw\s+\$\d+,%lo\(g\)`. It matches ours (before) and is absent in the after.

## Mechanism
In pass group `cse_expr`, sched and cse ask alias.c true_dependence. A varying `/s` (MEM_IN_STRUCT_P) access never conflicts with a fixed scalar (fixed_scalar_and_varying_struct_p). A store through a cast on top of a byte sum is not `/s`. So a global load must stay below it while unrelated insns (here the `lui %hi(g)`) float. Cite: `gcc/alias.c:1174 fixed_scalar_and_varying_struct_p` (staged tree refs/gcc-2.95.2-psx/). Map: docs/codegen-map/cse_expr.md K§4 (K§4b, K§4c) and sched.md S3.

## Lever
The lever is the source's aliasing facts. Spell the store as a typed index/member (`p[1] = v`, same address and `sw`). This grants `/s` and lets the load of the global float above. `*(int *)((char *)p + 4) = v` or a bare `*p =` denies it and pins the load below.
