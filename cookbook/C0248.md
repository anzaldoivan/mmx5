# C0248 — try-match but gate r6 red on lui/lo bytes: global store order, check objdump -dr relocs
tags: gate,try,reloc,store-order,global,lui,sha,objdump · date: 2026-10-25 · phase/task: 3.3/T30 · origin: W52 FAF28_ovl49

symptom: a draft try-matches, but the gate fails it at r6 with the whole-binary SHA red. Only `lui` and `%lo` bytes differ, at two global stores.

cause: try masks relocations. Two stores of the same constant to two globals in the wrong order differ only in their relocation targets.

lever: compare the `objdump -dr` relocation sequence of the object against target.s. Reorder the global stores until the sequences agree. A naive swap guess may not follow the scheduler, so a permutation search over the stores helps.

## Instance
W52 FAF28_ovl49 (c49 gate), 6 bytes at 0x13D740..0x13D75F. The lui/sb pairs of D_8009A42C and D_800D50DC were swapped. Writing `D_8009A42C = 0` first banked it at the re-gate.
Related: C0137 (try masks relocations: missing symbols), C0234 (store order).

## Byte proof
Gate (T30.c49, gate52rx3): rockx5 func_800FAF28_ovl49 banked r2 with the whole-binary SHA green, commit c583a892. A rider cannot show it, since try masks relocations.
