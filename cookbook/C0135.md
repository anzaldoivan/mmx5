# C0135 — Prove a source rewriter on a full wave, not only selftest fixtures
tags: porter,rewriter,selftest,shapes · date: 2026-10-17 · phase/task: 2.8/T13 · origin: generalizable

A member-path rewriter that slices off a prefix can drop one character (`->_pos`). A selftest fixture missed it. Running shapes + readability over a full wave of output caught it. Prove a rewriter on a real wave, not only fixtures. Source: 2.8 T13.
