# C0174 — Constant store in the wrong register: assign it to the compared local, store the local
tags: codegen,regalloc,v0-v1,constant-store,local,reuse · date: 2026-10-20 · phase/task: 3.3/T8 · origin: W30 FC3F8

symptom: a constant stored after a compare takes the other register of the pair (`li 0x32` in `$v1`, not `$v0`). The rest of the arm matches.
lever: assign the constant to the local that already holds the compared field, then store the local: `state = 0x32; obj->unk7C = state;`.

## Residual
W30 FC3F8_ovl38: with `s32 state = arg0->unk45` already in place (try 3), `arg0->unk7C = 0x32;` leaves diff 2 of 35 words. That local serves the compare and the scratchpad store.

## Lever
`state = 0x32; arg0->unk7C = state;` matches: the constant takes the local's register.
Related: C0076 (constant stores alternate v0/v1). Try this one when a single constant store in an arm sits in the wrong register.
