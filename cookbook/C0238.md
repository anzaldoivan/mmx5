# C0238 — Fail store placed before the call arm: one || chain with the assignment inside, not nested ifs
tags: codegen,block-layout,or-chain,assignment,early-return,if-else,guard · date: 2026-10-25 · phase/task: 3.3/T28 · origin: W50 F2E48_ovl02

symptom: a store that runs when any of three guards fails is laid out after the call arm. The target places it first and jumps over it to the call arm.

lever: fold the guards into one `||` chain and do the assignment inside it, as in `(c = (s8)D) == 0`. Put the fail store in the then-arm and the call arm in the else-arm.

## Instance
W50 F2E48_ovl02 (c23): nested ifs with an early return after the call and the fail store last gave diff 15 of 28 words. The single `||` chain matched at try 2.
Related: C0175 (guards as early returns, the opposite layout), C0151 (range tests in one condition).
