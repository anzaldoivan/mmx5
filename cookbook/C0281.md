# C0281 — Word select with one shared ori after the join: select the high half, OR the low half
tags: codegen,constant,lui,ori,select,join,if-else,split · date: 2026-11-01 · phase/task: 3.4/T9 · origin: W74 F0CDC_ovl05

symptom: a two-constant word select stores `0x28000` or `0xFFFD8000`. The target has one `lui` per arm, with one in a delay slot. Then one shared `ori 0x8000` and one `sw` follow the join. Ours loads each whole constant per arm with its own `lui` and `ori`.

cause: gcc emits a 32-bit constant as `lui` plus `ori` inside each arm. A shared low half that the source applies after the join becomes one `ori` on the joined register.

lever: select only the high half, then OR the common low half at the store. Write `v = 0x20000; if (c) v = 0xFFFD0000; *(s32*)(obj + 0x24) = v | 0x8000;`.
